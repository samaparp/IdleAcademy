# Notes

The tier-1 resource of [[Idle Academy]], produced by [[Study]], tier 1 of the [[Research]] category.

> [!abstract] Summary
> **+1 Note** per Study tick, including at max level.

- Produced by: [[Study]], 1 per tick.
- Consumed by: **nothing yet.**
- Defined in `js/config.js` under `CONFIG.resources.notes`.

> [!question] What are Notes for?
> Nothing spends Notes yet. Until something does, the number only goes up.
> Whatever consumes them (crafting, unlocking [[Sketch]], or a system not
> yet designed) will set how fast they should accrue.

> [!note] Naming
> Renamed from the placeholder **Insight**. First rung of the [[Research]]
> ladder: Notes → Sketches → Blueprints → Treatises.
