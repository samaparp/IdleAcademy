# Notes

The tier-1 resource of [[Idle Academy]], produced by [[Research]].

> [!abstract] Summary
> **+1 Note** per Research tick, including at max level.

- Produced by: [[Research]], 1 per tick.
- Consumed by: **nothing yet.**
- Defined in `js/config.js` under `CONFIG.resources.notes`.

> [!question] What are Notes for?
> Nothing spends Notes yet. Until something does, the number only goes up.
> Whatever consumes them (crafting, unlocking higher Research tiers, or a
> system not yet designed) will set how fast they should accrue.

> [!note] Naming
> Renamed from the placeholder **Insight**. Sits on a scholarly ladder whose
> next rung is proposed as **Sketches** — see the open questions in
> [[Research]].
