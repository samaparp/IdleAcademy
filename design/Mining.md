# Mining

The second skill the player reaches, and the end of the [[Opening]] chain.

> [!abstract] Summary
> Dug by hand with a pickaxe, at a mine in **Arjunia**, near but outside
> Rupina. The first metal is **copper**.

## Where

Arjunia is the kingdom's **extraction and primary metalworking** region —
the raw and the refined, sent south to be manufactured. The mine sits with
the works at the mountain foot, kilometres from anywhere anyone lives, as
the world describes them. See [[Geography]].

> [!note] Not the Silver Peaks
> That lode is **silver, and exhausted**, with the mine-owners' mansions
> standing empty above it. Arjunia's industry is alive; only that one
> seam ran dry. It stays available as a later hook rather than a first
> mine.

> [!note] The economy points the same way
> Arjunia extracts, the south manufactures. The player **digs in Arjunia
> and builds at home in [[Stennard]]**, which is exactly the trade route
> the world already runs.

## Copper first

Copper is the right first metal on every axis:

- **Historically.** The first metal humans worked. Soft, low melting
  point, workable by one person with a bench rather than an industrial
  furnace — which is the whole of the [[Character]]'s means.
- **For the setting.** Brass is copper and zinc, and brass is what 1888
  instruments, lanterns, fittings and casings are made of. Copper leads
  straight to the material this game's objects are built from.
- **For the ladder.** It opens an obvious progression — copper alone,
  then copper plus a second ore for an alloy, then iron and steel when
  the player has a furnace worth the name.

> [!question] Undecided

> - **What comes after copper** — tin for bronze, zinc for brass, or
>   straight to iron. Brass is the more 1888 answer and the more
>   steampunk one; bronze is archaic by this period.
> - Tick length, XP and yield — see [[Skill Model]].
> - Whether the pickaxe must **stay** equipped, and whether tools wear
