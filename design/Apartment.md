# Apartment

The [[Character]]'s room — a **sub-location inside [[Stennard]]**, and the
only place he owns.

> [!abstract] Summary
> Unlocked by default. Has **its own tab**. Holds **furniture**, which is
> **upgraded** with [[Quid]] and materials.

## Furniture

Each piece is a workstation, upgradeable in place.

| Furniture        | Enables                                   | State      |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| **Writing desk** | Writing — the first craftable, see [[Opening]] | Named only |
| **Workbench**    | Assembling equipment                      | Named only |
| **Laboratory**   | Alchemy                                   | Named only |

> [!important] This is the first confirmed sink for [[Quid]]
> Until now quid had a source and nothing to spend it on. Furniture
> upgrades are a money sink that feeds directly back into production rate
> or capability — the classic idle-game loop.

> [!important] Crafting happens at home, not in town
> The desk, workbench and lab are **apartment furniture**. That makes the
> apartment the crafting hub, which changes what the town is for.

> [!question] So what is the Stennard workshop for?
> [[Stennard]] lists a **workshop** as a venue, and the apartment now has
> a **workbench**. Either one is redundant, or they are tiers — a cramped
> bench at home, a proper workshop in town for bigger work. **Undecided.**
> The same question applies to the apothecary and the home laboratory.
