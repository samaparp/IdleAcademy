# Stennard

The starting location of [[Idle Academy]], and the town the [[Character]]
works out of.

> [!abstract] Summary
> **Unlocked by default.** Everything except the mine is here, including
> the player's apartment. The **market** and the **Labour Exchange** are
> open from the start; the rest is not yet decided.

## Venues

| Venue          | Purpose                                              | State       |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| **Market**     | Buying and selling. The main source of [[Quid]]      | Open from the start. **On the critical path** |
| **Workshop**   | Assembling things from research and materials        | **On the critical path** — see [[Opening]] |
| **Labour Exchange** | Job offers — see [[Jobs]]                       | **Open from the start. On the critical path** |
| **Apartment**  | The player's room                                    | **Open from the start** |
| **Apothecary** | Alchemy. Explicitly later                            | Proposed    |

> [!important] Assembly is step two, not a later system
> [[Opening]] puts assembling and selling in the first few minutes. The
> market and a place to assemble are both needed before anything else in
> the game can happen.

The **mine** is *not* in Stennard — it sits in another region. See
[[Locations]].

## Naming

The designer asked what 1888 would call a forge and a tavern. These are
**proposals, not decisions**.

> [!tip] Workshop, not forge
> **Forge** reads fantasy-RPG. **Workshop** is period, plain, and pairs
> naturally with Blueprints — you draft a thing, then you build it at the
> bench. Alternatives with more colour: **the Works**, **machine shop**,
> or **foundry** if it is specifically casting metal.

> [!tip] Public house, not tavern
> "Tavern" is medieval, or American. In 1888 Britain it is a **public
> house** — "pub" was already everyday speech. Others, each with a
> different flavour:
> - **Gin palace** — ornate, gas-lit, mirrored, loud. Intensely Victorian
>   and specifically a drinking place rather than an inn.
> - **Alehouse** — humbler, older-fashioned by 1888.
> - **Inn** — implies lodging and a coaching yard.
>
> The most period move is to skip the category and **name the pub**: a
> hunter drinks at The Black Dog, not at "the tavern".

> [!tip] Two more worth reconsidering, unasked
> - **Guild** has the same fantasy problem as forge. The 1888 word is
>   **Society** — and the real Society for Psychical Research was founded
>   in **1882**, which is almost exactly this game's premise. **Institute**
>   or **Association** also work.
> - **Alchemy** is archaic by 1888. An **apothecary** sells preparations;
>   a **chemist** is the British high-street version; a **laboratory** is
>   where the work happens. Apothecary is the most atmospheric.
